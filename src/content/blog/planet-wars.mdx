You remember this game even if you don't remember its name. Circles on a screen. Yours are one color. You click a circle, click another circle, and half your little army flies across the void. Galcon, Phage Wars, Tentacle Wars, Auralux — the whole Flash-era genre.

Here's the thing nobody tells you: in 2010, Google ran an entire AI programming competition on exactly this game. It's a *genuinely hard* AI problem — no turns, simultaneous moves, troops in flight. Minimax, the engine behind every chess and Connect 4 bot, is useless here.

But first, play it. You're indigo, starting on the left. Bigger planets produce troops faster. Neutral gray planets don't produce anything until somebody pays the entry fee.
